Structured Reflection

Structured Post-Activity Debrief

Most providers stop when the games end. We go beyond that. Every engagement ends with a structured debrief that transforms activities into meaningful insights, helping teams recognize strengths, address challenges, and apply what they have learned to the way they work together.

01 · 5 MINUTES

REST & REACT

What did you notice?

Participants share observations about the activity and their own responses in a relaxed, low-pressure setting. Sharing personal experiences is always optional.

02 · 15 MINUTES

SO WHAT

What did it reveal about how we work?

The team connects the activity to workplace dynamics such as communication, trust, collaboration, and accountability, uncovering patterns that influence performance.

03 · 10 MINUTES

NOW WHAT

What changes on Monday?

Each team identifies specific, achievable actions to apply its insights at work, turning reflection into practical commitments.

30 MINUTES TOTAL · REFLECT → CONNECT → COMMIT
